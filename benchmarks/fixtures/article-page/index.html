Refunds and returns

This article collects the questions support receives most often. It is long because the policy is long, and most of it does not answer the one question a person usually has when they open it.

When a refund arrives

A refund appears on the original payment method five to ten business days after the return is scanned by the courier. The window is set by the card network rather than by the shop, which is why support cannot shorten it and why the number on a statement can lag the email that announced it.

Nothing else changes the timing: not the amount, not the reason for the return, not whether the parcel arrived at a depot or a shop counter. The refund is issued once and appears once, and a second email about the same refund is a notification rather than a second payment.

Return windows

An order can be returned within thirty days of delivery. The window opens when the parcel is delivered, not when it was ordered, so an order placed in one month and delivered in the next gets its thirty days from the day the tracking says it arrived.

Items bought in a sale follow the same rule. The only exception is an item that was made to order, which carries the window stated on its own page, because it cannot be resold once it has been cut.

What can be returned

Anything unused, in its packaging, with its labels attached. That includes gifts received from someone else, which can be returned for store credit without the buyer being involved.

Some items cannot come back for hygiene or safety reasons: opened cosmetics, pierced jewellery, perishable food, and anything that has been cut to size. These are listed on the item page before the order is placed, and the list is the same in every region where the shop trades.

Return costs

A return costs nothing when the item was faulty or when the wrong item was sent. Otherwise the cost of the label is deducted from the refund, and the amount is shown before the label is issued rather than after.

Collection from an address costs more than dropping a parcel at a shop, because the courier charges for the visit. Both options use the same tracking and the same thirty-day window.

Exchanges

An exchange is a return followed by a new order, which means the new order is charged immediately and the refund for the old one follows the timing above. Sizes and colours can be exchanged this way; nothing else can.

Reserving an exchange item for the duration of a return is not something the shop can do, so an item that sells out between the two orders cannot be exchanged into, only refunded.

Damaged or faulty items

A damaged item is replaced if a photograph is supplied within thirty days of delivery. The photograph is used to decide the outcome, so the parcel does not have to come back first.

A fault that appears later is covered by the manufacturer rather than by the shop once the return window has closed, and the manufacturer's process is usually the faster route at that point.

Missing parcels

A parcel that tracking says was delivered but is not where it should be is treated as missing after twenty-four hours. Support asks for the delivery address once, then either finds the parcel or refunds it.

A parcel still in transit after the estimated date is not missing, and a refund cannot be issued for it until the carrier closes the investigation.

Gift returns

A gift can be returned for store credit without a receipt, provided the sender did not mark the order as non-returnable. Store credit does not expire and can be used against any order.

International orders

Returns from outside the country the order was placed in are charged the outbound shipping as well as the return label, because neither leg can be recovered from the carrier.

Changes to this policy

The policy is reviewed twice a year. A change applies to orders placed after the change, never to orders already placed, and the date at the top of this page is the date of the last change.

Contacting support

Support answers in the order messages arrive. A message that includes the order number and a photograph is answered faster than one that asks a question the article above already answers, which is the reason the article is here.
